# Living home and guppy stock

Implements release-order slice 2 of final-product.md. Launch into a safe planted home aquarium containing the actual kept individuals. Empty saves show one clear first-guppy action; unfinished outings offer Continue raising. Home viewing and free swimming never consume residents, advance hunger or replace the active outing.

Raise opens a stock sheet with free Ordinary, 40-credit Sunburst and 75-credit Blue Veil guppies. Ordinary has a silver/warm body and short translucent fins; paid stock has distinct inherited colour/fin form visible in preview and play. Show current starting condition, inherited form, price, growth potential, small-fish diet and Guppy Garden destination. Potential is distinct from the existing acquired Swift/Ornamental choices; no new progression or breeding mechanics in this slice.

Use the existing atomic profile/key with additive fields and sanitised migration: kept residents have a home placement; legacy colours, acquired traits, coins, completed IDs and active positions are retained. Starting stock debits and creates the active run in one write, checks funds, existing run and completed IDs, and rejects replay. Failed storage leaves the purchase uncommitted and the current outing available in memory. No instant resale reward for untouched stock.

React owns home, stock sheet and outing visibility. Keep a live outing mounted and paused while visiting home to preserve unsaved motion if storage fails. Sale/keep receipts lead to home or stock choice; never silently spawn another fish. One shared audio hook owned by App serves all screens; preserve the existing music/effects implementation with clean disposal.

Home reuses existing procedural fish/plant/rock rendering. Every resident has a persistent identity, palette, pose and wander state. A selected resident can be controlled in all directions inside home, with no predator, food or transactions; return to watching without changing owned state. Bound visible simulation to 24 residents and show total count and collection pages for larger legacy saves. No runtime dependencies.

Acceptance: migrated reload, empty/populated home, stock previews, insufficient funds, duplicate/replayed purchase, keep placement, paused outing resume, storage failure, safe controlled resident and nursery sale/keep loop. Inspect portrait and short viewport screenshots; do not claim physical-device/audio performance.
