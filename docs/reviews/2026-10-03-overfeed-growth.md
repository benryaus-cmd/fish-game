# Overfeeding growth trade-off

Eligible meals now grant up to 15 healthy seconds even above 85 food, bounded by the 600-second adulthood requirement. The existing overfeeding health loss (maximum 6), nutrition policy, diet restrictions, death handling and elapsed age floor remain. Notices show seconds saved plus health loss; when the elapsed age floor masks immediate savings they show banked growth credit instead of a misleading zero. Adult fish have no next-stage notice.

Verification: the full-fish regression failed with zero credit before the change, then passed with 15. Full suite: 164 passed, two optional native export skips, no failures. Scoped TypeScript and production build passed. Built-browser overfeed journey confirmed a full juvenile loses health and displays 15s saved to adult, with no page errors.
