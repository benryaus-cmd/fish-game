## Current priorities

The presentation, living home/stock, care-world and exotic aquarium slices are implemented. Read [exotic aquarium evidence](docs/reviews/2026-10-02-exotic-aquarium.md), [care-world evidence](docs/reviews/2026-10-02-care-world.md), [the footage review](docs/reviews/2026-10-02-footage-review.md) and [home/stock implementation](docs/reviews/2026-10-02-home-stock-implementation.md). The living-aquarium slice adds View/Swim of one persistent aquarium, deliberate overfeeding risk, castle healing, roaming ecology and care-derived upgrades. Read docs/reviews/2026-10-02-living-aquarium.md before continuing. Next is a small tracked FishoDex and meaningful habitat expansion. Builds and behaviour tests alone do not establish visual quality. The [living-swimming review](docs/reviews/2026-10-02-living-swimming.md) distinguishes relaxed passive aquarium motion from controlled back-facing dives; do not reintroduce binary side-facing vertical poses.

The saved home aquarium is the main menu. Kept fish are persistent individuals and breeding stock. Nine stocks use starter, colourful tropical, native seahorse and original native angel anatomy. Seahorse appears under Tropical; Rainbow Angel is the third Angels option. Seahorses breed with seahorses; existing fish cross-family rules remain. Breeding can combine body, hue, pattern, fin silhouette and tail independently under fictional game rules; acquired Ornamental/Athletic/Vitality choices stay separate. Preserve the narrow legacy fin exception: old legacy fish without new bodyShape retain their previous silhouette, but newly bred legacy-origin offspring must render inherited tails. Care influences acquired colour quality and reversible current vibrancy without changing hereditary hue. A two-minute nursery cycle reserves two adults and one space, persists offspring at start and claims once. Every tank uses turquoise day/petrol night endpoints; phases last five minutes each. Care uses saved device timestamps, not a server clock; adulthood starts from ten healthy minutes plus nutrition; worthwhile meals grant bounded credit with a 450-second elapsed floor. Deliberate EAT supports pointer/hold/E/native keyboard activation; do not restore collision eating. Original articulated shrimp are the only size-gated live food; guppies never eat other fish. Crabs roam outside the nursery, telegraph and hop. Castle bubbles heal without safety. At zero health, including owned individuals, persist death, show a four-second upside-down float, then remove once. View predators never attack. Paid View pellets cost 5 and cleaning 15; Swim cleaning and foraging are free. Preserve queued choices while visibly present; away crossings resolve deterministically. Four choices are speed, vibrancy, fins and vitality. Adjustable habitats and FishoDex remain intended systems.

## Implementation guardrails

- Preserve procedural fish character. Pose the entire body/fin frame consistently and inspect steep dives, climbs and both turn directions; do not conceal geometry errors with unconditional fin overlays.
- Keep gameplay centre clear. Guidance is brief or at the edges. Zoom updates world bounds/culling/effects together while HUD and touch targets retain their size.
- Keep inherited traits, acquired development, current condition and environmental modifiers distinct. Species remain species; breeding compatibility and environmental effects are explicit data.
- Preserve coins, kept fish and active runs through save migrations. Purchases, sales, transfers and breeding outcomes commit once; no refresh rerolls or lost individuals.
- Maintain one audio owner as screens multiply. Profile before renderer/library changes; measure on mobile before claiming performance targets.
- Use drawSpecimenFish/specimenMouthPoint for owned fish so native angel anatomy and eating agree. Keep background/decor caches bounded: home blends baked endpoints; growing water/sand blend continuously and decor uses three palette levels. Palette caches use derived colours, not per-frame specimen object identity.
- Verify the changed player journey in the built app, with screenshots or footage for visual changes. State device/audio checks not performed. Do not repeatedly rerun passing checks without a changed reason.
- Publish scoped GitHub updates through the established Node/Vite import manifest with a direct Aippy prompt when product code changes. Documentation changes need no game import. Never describe planned features or unexecuted imports as completed.

Current name is SWIM. Preserve legacy aqualume save keys for migration. Ten owned slots include active fish and reserved offspring; never delete legacy oversized collections. Sales are available at any stage. Return to View mode requires nursery presence; View must keep animating after Swim receipts. Visual pitch is capped at 50° from side-on without limiting vertical movement. Audio sliders persist at 80% music / 60% SFX defaults.

View shows both neutral crabs, route rocks and small seagrass, with a castle 70% larger than its earlier overview size. Swim has low seagrass patches across the route. Crab warning raises one claw; its hop raises both. Overfeeding notice explicitly reports actual time credit (currently zero) alongside health loss.

View has a Buy fish dock action. Purchases or selection changes park the previous active individual atomically, preserving identity/care and ownership; all10-slot checks remain. Selected-fish Swim action sits directly under its identity. Anatomy labels are Guppy/Tropical/Angelfish/Seahorse form, while save identifiers are unchanged. Deliberately caught shrimp give1 saved credit and disclose it in meal notices. Burst release has1s cooldown, zero stamina locks until strictly above20%, under20% is red, recovery is11/s moving or19/s resting (half previous). Predators use the player50° visual pitch cap and continuous3D yaw, with stronger charges. SDK vibration accompanies actual damage; View stays neutral. Water gets bounded viewport grains, soft ripples and deeper cached rays.

Predator/crab balance: both deal 8 health out of 100 per hit. Predator detection radius is 600 world units (80% of its former 750). Ending a chase preserves the current heading toward one fixed destination while slowing to patrol speed; do not choose retreat targets every frame or force a turnaround.
Default Burst endurance is 50% longer (normalized drain 65/1.5 per second); retain the saved 0–100 stamina scale and existing recovery/cooldown thresholds.

Launch welcomes players with V0.9, the native Rainbow portrait and a concise how-to-play card. Dismiss into View; returning from Swim does not reopen it. Browser journeys should dismiss Let’s begin before interacting with Home.
